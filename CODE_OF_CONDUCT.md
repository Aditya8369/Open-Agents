# Code of Conduct

## Our Promise

We want Open-Agents to be a welcoming place for everyone.
No matter who you are, where you're from, or how much experience you have —
you deserve to be treated with respect here.

## What We Expect

Be kind. Be helpful. Be patient.

Good behavior looks like:

- Being friendly and welcoming to new contributors
- Respecting different opinions and ideas
- Giving honest, helpful feedback
- Owning your mistakes and learning from them

Bad behavior looks like:

- Insulting or attacking other people
- Harassment of any kind
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Any behavior that makes others feel unwelcome or unsafe

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub repos, issues,
pull requests, discussions — as well as in public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban at the
maintainers' discretion, depending on severity.

## If Something Goes Wrong

If someone is behaving badly, please report it privately to the maintainers.
Open a private security advisory or contact the email listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Every report is taken seriously and handled fairly and confidentially.
